Artificial Intelligence (AI) represents a paradigm shift in human capability, enabling computer systems to imitate cognitive functions such as learning, reasoning, pattern recognition, and problem-solving. By processing vast datasets at unprecedented speeds, modern AI models can automate complex workflows, drive advanced research in areas like medicine and cybersecurity, while also improving daily productivity through the use of intuitive digital assistants. As these technologies continue to evolve, they have great potential to address diverse global issues, but their rapid growth also brings significant debates about responsible integration into society.
