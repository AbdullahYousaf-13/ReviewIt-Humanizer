It is important to note that AI plagiarism checkers are not completely reliable, and they should always be used with caution, as they can sometimes make mistakes and incorrectly identify genuine human writing as content that was generated by artificial intelligence.
In addition, these findings clearly highlight the importance of being careful when interpreting the results of AI-detection tools, as relying on them too heavily could lead to unfair or inaccurate conclusions about students' work (Perkins et al., 2024).
