## What was run

- **Model / settings:** `gemini-3.8-flash`, the single prompt in `gemini_prompt.py`, system instruction, temperature 1.0, thinking level low, 3 rewrites per call; each sentence got its real neighbouring sentences as context.
- **Calls:** exactly **67** — Academic on all 27 sentences (24 flagged + 3 mixed), then Business, Casual, Creative, Narrative and Technical on the same 8 flagged sentences each (3 with citations, 5 without). One call at a time, no retries.
- **Real cost: $0.0633** for 67 calls ($0.00094 per call; estimate was about $0.13). Price used: $0.75 / 1M input and $3.75 / 1M output tokens (paid tier, until 31 Dec 2026).
- **Time per call:** mean 3.4 s, fastest 1.8 s, slowest 14.6 s (median 2.8 s).
- **Recommended rewrite** = what the app would show as "Recommended": the lowest-scoring of Gemini's 3 rewrites that passes the fact guard and scores below the original.
- **AI score** = our BERT detector (`followsci/bert-ai-text-detector`), single-sentence. **Evaded** = score below 40%. This is *our* detector, not ZeroGPT or Copyleaks.
- **Meaning kept** = the automatic guard (numbers, citations kept once and not added, negation, direction of the claim, length) **plus my own read** of original vs rewrite. ✔ = kept, ⚠ = a change worth a look. Not a human expert's judgement.
- **Test cap:** this test limited each reply to 700 tokens. **4 calls hit it** and came back short (marked †): Academic #21, Casual #118, Technical #20 (cut mid-sentence), Technical #75. The app sets no cap, so these would normally complete.

## What the results show

1. **Evasion on our detector:** in Academic style Gemini produced a kept rewrite for 18 of 27 sentences and 12 of those scored below 40%. For 9 sentences (Academic #23, #26, #28, #30, #31, #32, #35, #136 and the truncated #21) there is no kept rewrite: for 8 of them all 3 rewrites still scored 100%, mostly list-style or definition sentences.
2. **Style barely changes the output.** The 6 styles give very similar sentences (only Gemini applies a style, and only through one line of the prompt). This matches open issue B9.
3. **Meaning:** most recommended rewrites keep the meaning. The ⚠ rows are mostly small drops ("For example", "and for promotion") or softened wording; two Narrative rows add a link or claim that is not in the original (Narrative #20 and #22). The context sentences make Gemini add linking words, which the guard cannot catch.
4. **Guard gaps found:** (a) a reply cut off mid-sentence (Technical #20) passes the guard; (b) a citation rewritten from `X (2023)` to `(X and Y, 2023)` changes how it reads but is allowed on purpose.
5. **T5** mostly returns near-copies of the original (13 of its 14 accepted rewrites share 85% or more of the original's words), which is why it scores low. **Humarin** gives real paraphrases but only for 11 of 27 sentences.
6. **Speed and cost:** about 3 seconds and under a tenth of a cent per call.
