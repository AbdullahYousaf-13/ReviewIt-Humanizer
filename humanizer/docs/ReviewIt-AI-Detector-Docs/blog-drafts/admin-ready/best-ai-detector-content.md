## What is an AI detector, and who needs one?

An AI detector is a tool that reads a piece of writing and shows how much of it reads as machine-written. People use one for different reasons:

- **Students** check an essay or dissertation chapter before they submit it.
- **Academics and researchers** review a manuscript, a literature review or a grant application.
- **Editors and teachers** want a quick second opinion on a piece of text.
- **Writers** who use AI tools for ideas want to see where their own voice is missing.

Whatever the reason, the goal is the same: to understand your own text better. That is why the best AI detector is not the one with the loudest promise. It is the one that gives you the clearest picture and the most useful next step.

## What makes the best AI detector? Five things to look for

Before you trust any AI detector, check it against these five points.

### 1. Sentence-level results, not a single score

A score for a whole document is hard to act on. If a tool says your paper is "40% AI", you have no idea where to start. The best AI detector shows you which sentences read as AI-written, so you can look at them one by one. A map beats a number every time.

### 2. Accuracy you can understand

Every tool claims to be accurate. Look for a clear figure, a clear statement of what kind of writing it applies to, and an honest note that no detector is perfect. A detector tuned for a particular kind of writing, such as academic writing, will usually serve that kind of writing better than a generic one.

### 3. Respect for citations and references

Academic text is full of citations, reference lists, headings and formulas. A detector that treats them like ordinary sentences will flag text that no one wrote with a machine. The best AI detector for academic work understands that these parts are different.

### 4. Privacy and control over your data

You are handing over your work. Check whether your text is used to train anything, whether you can delete your results, and what happens to uploaded files. A good tool is open about all three.

### 5. Clear next steps

A result is only useful if you can do something with it. The best AI detector helps you move from "this sentence is flagged" to "here is how to improve it", ideally without leaving the tool.
